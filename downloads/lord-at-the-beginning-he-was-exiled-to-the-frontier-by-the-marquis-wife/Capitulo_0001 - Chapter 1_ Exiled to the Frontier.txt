Chapter 1: Exiled to the Frontier

In the northern part of the Frostmoon Province, Alpha Kingdom, inside an ordinary carriage traveling along a wide road, a pale-faced Levi lay in agony.

Strange images flashed continuously through his mind, painful as if he were having a "nightmare" from which he could not wake.

Feeling as though he had spent an eternity in that "nightmare," Levi struggled desperately to break free, jolting upright with a start, gasping for air, his head drenched in sweat.

"Young Master... you... are awake... How are you... are you alright..."

"Huh?"

After steadying his emotions, Levi stared blankly at the old man who was muttering incessantly before him.

"Who is this old foreign geezer?"

Just as Levi was about to ask, suddenly, "Ouch!" The carriage plunged into a large pit, and Levi was jolted, hitting his head.

"What is going on?"

"Young Master, the carriage is stuck in a pit and cannot get out. We need to get down and let Walker pull the carriage out; it is also a good chance for you to get some fresh air," the old man said.

At this moment, Levi suddenly felt something was wrong. "A carriage?"

He hurriedly ran off the carriage, only to be met with a desolate scene. Seeing soldiers in armor guarding the surroundings around the carriage, Levi was instantly overwhelmed.

"Who am I? Where am I? What am I doing?"

Looking at the scene around him, Levi could not help but ask himself these three soul-searching questions.

He remembered being dumped by his girlfriend last night, drinking too much out of distress, then seemingly getting into a conflict with someone, getting smashed in the head with a bottle, and then remembering nothing else.

Just as Levi was struggling to recall, a flood of new memories suddenly surged into his mind. As he integrated these new memories,

a sturdy man in black armor rode over on a tall horse. After performing a knight's salute, he said: "Baron, this place is not safe. We should leave quickly!"

"Huh? Then let us go quickly. Walker, have you gotten the carriage out yet?"

Hearing the man's warning, the old man who had been chattering incessantly beside Levi became instantly nervous and urged the coachman frantically.

"It is ready, it is ready, Baron, you can get back in the carriage!"

A moment later, the coachman Walker managed to get the carriage wheels out of the dirt pit. The old man immediately pulled the still-dazed Levi back into the carriage.

Half an hour later, Levi finally understood his current situation. "...It is not a dream... I have transmigrated!"

After integrating the memories, Levi learned that this was a world called Noah Continent, a place where many races coexisted, including humans, beastmen, elves, dwarves, sea folk, undead, and even dragons.

This world also possessed extraordinary professions such as knights, warriors, and mages; it was an extraordinary world. It was also a world plagued by war, with large-scale conflicts frequently erupting between races, and internal civil wars often occurring between human nobles.

However, the original owner only had information about his own kingdom and its surroundings.

The country where the original owner resided was called Alpha Kingdom. It was one of the more powerful kingdoms in the vicinity, having risen over a thousand years ago, and was located roughly in the northeast of the continent.

To the east lay the East Sea, though the ocean was inhabited by sea folk and Naga, so humans rarely ventured there.

To the north was a stretch of the Barren Mountains running east-west for thousands of kilometers, serving as the natural boundary between Alpha Kingdom, several human kingdoms to the west, and the Beastman Great Plains.

The Beastman Empire was located on the Beastman Great Plains. Almost every three to five years, they would cross the Barren Mountains to invade Alpha Kingdom and its neighboring countries, pillaging resources and people before immediately returning to the Beastman Great Plains.

It was currently the year 6637 of the Dawn Calendar. It was said that Alpha Kingdom and its neighboring countries all worshipped the Goddess of Dawn, so everyone's calendar was calculated from the day the Goddess of Dawn descended.

In other words, this world might also possess the existence of deities, though the original owner clearly could not access information at that level.

The social system of human kingdoms in this world was mostly a rigid feudal system of slavery and nobility, where nobles enjoyed everything and the rest were enslaved. Fortunately, the original owner was a noble.

The original owner of this body was also named Levi, full name Levi Louis, and was the second son of a small baronial family in the south of Alpha Kingdom.

The original owner Levi's father, the Baron, had passed away suddenly not long ago. After his elder brother inherited the title, the 16-year-old Levi, unwilling to join the army, was driven out of the Baron Louis Territory by his brother.

Afterwards, the original owner Levi brought his personal old servant, Old Charlie, to Tulip City, hoping to find a way out in this city of commerce.

Unexpectedly, he caught the eye of the owner of Tulip City, the kingdom's famous beauty, the Tulip Duchess Angelis. Because the original owner indeed possessed a handsome "appearance," he, with his striking good looks, was immediately "married into the Duchess's estate."

However, at the wedding banquet a month ago, the original owner seemed to have gotten carried away, becoming dead drunk and throwing a drunken tantrum, turning a perfectly good wedding banquet into a joke and causing the Tulip Duchess to lose face completely.

This greatly enraged the Tulip Duchess Angelis, who immediately gave Levi a baronial title and a fief, exiling him, though the marital relationship was not dissolved.

However, a baronial title and a fief were things the original owner Levi could not have earned in a lifetime, so he was not too saddened and soon excitedly headed to the royal capital to register his title and fief.

But when he learned that his fief was actually on the northeastern border of the kingdom, on the front line of the battlefield against the beastmen, the original owner Levi was instantly frightened into a faint, and subsequently passed away in his confused slumber, which was when he was replaced by the Levi from Blue Star who had been beaten to death after drinking.

Levi immediately smiled bitterly in his heart: "We really are brothers in misfortune, both losing our lives because of women and drinking. Sigh, I hope you have a decent transmigration journey too."

After understanding his situation and adjusting slightly, Levi had no choice but to accept the fact of his transmigration. What else could he do? He could not just go for another drink and transmigrate back; what if he ended up paralyzed? That would be miserable.

Besides, he was now a handsome noble baron in a brilliant and colorful extraordinary world, so naturally, he had to experience it properly.

As for the dangers of this world and his current predicament, ha, that was nothing. Could it be harder than the Huanggang test papers? Could it be more bitter than working 997? Could it be more dangerous than inflated housing prices and vain women?

After giving himself a pep talk and encouragement in his heart, Levi set a small goal for himself—to survive. ಥ _ ಥ
