Levi looked at the panel and the relevant introduction, feeling incredibly excited.

"According to what is introduced here, I only need to increase the skill value of the Flame Breathing Technique to 101, and it can awaken the Life Seed, breaking through directly to become an official knight without needing the precious Life Source Liquid."

Because according to the memories of the original owner, Life Source Liquid was a very precious item, controlled by the great nobles and the royal family, and could only be obtained in exchange for military merit.

And for a knight to awaken the Life Seed, Life Source Liquid was mandatory; otherwise, it was impossible to stimulate the potential within the body, and thus impossible to give birth to extraordinary power.

Of course, it seemed some monstrous geniuses could break through on their own.

"And I can obtain one attribute point every day. That is to say, as long as I live long enough, there is no level I cannot break through to, be it Sky Knight, Saint Knight, or even Legendary."

The nine levels of official knights are, respectively: Primary Knight, Intermediate Knight, Senior Knight, Great Knight, Silver Knight, Gold Knight, Earth Knight, Sky Knight, Saint Knight, and above the nine levels, there is the Legendary Knight.

"And this innate talent—the panel can actually awaken innate talents!

I heard that among hundreds of knights, it is only possible for one 'Chosen One' to be born and awaken an innate talent.

I haven't even promoted to an official knight yet, and I already possess an innate talent, and I can continue to awaken more in the future.

It seems no one in the Alpha Kingdom has ever appeared who has awakened more than two innate talents!"

Thinking to the end, Levi could not help but burst into loud laughter.

"Hahaha..."

"Young Master, Young Master, please calm down. Things will get better. Once we arrive at the Stormwind Territory, we will ask Lady Angelis for help; she will assist you."

Old Charlie thought Levi had been frightened again by matters regarding the territory, so he hurriedly tried to console Levi.

"Old Charlie, rest assured, I am fine. You don't need to worry too much about the problems with the territory; I will handle them."

Levi composed the mood and reassured Old Charlie.

Now that it had a cultivation cheat, what else was there to worry about?

Moreover, the first innate talent awakened was a life-saving talent for predicting danger.

This talent could allow it to foresee danger, such as the danger warning Levi was receiving now:

[In the shrubbery 1 kilometer ahead, there are 55 thieves lying in ambush, including one Tier 1 Primary Fighter, 5 elite soldiers, and the rest are a rabble!]

After understanding the content of the danger warning, Levi shouted:

"Old Walker, stop the carriage!"

Subsequently, under the puzzled gaze of Old Charlie, Levi stepped off the carriage.

Seeing Levi step off the carriage, Clay, the captain of the Knight Squad responsible for leading the way, immediately became somewhat dissatisfied.

"Baron, I said this place is not safe and that magical beasts might appear. If you want to rest, wait until we pass this shrubbery."

Seeing the attitude of Captain Clay, Levi was not angry, because this Knight Squad was originally knights of the Tulip Family, just arranged by Angelis to help Levi stabilize the new Stormwind Territory for a term of only two years.

It was just that what was originally an easy errand had been schemed by someone with ulterior motives, getting the territory of Levi moved to the dangerous Stormwind Cape, causing this Knight Squad to have to share in the misfortune of Levi.

It would be a wonder if they could have a good attitude toward Levi.

"Captain Clay, haven't you noticed that the shrubbery ahead is far too quiet?" Levi explained.

Hearing the explanation of Levi, Captain Clay was stunned, then immediately turned the head to look at the shrubbery on both sides of the road ahead.

It was indeed too quiet; there wasn't even a single bird.

Clay immediately discovered the problem, then turned the head back to look at Levi with some surprise, and immediately said apologetically:

"Apologies, Baron, it was my oversight!"

Clay then ordered:

"Roger, Sam, you two stay behind to protect the Baron. Everyone else, come with me to investigate."

After speaking, Clay led ten Apprentice Knights forward in a combat formation to investigate cautiously.

Levi actually wanted to go as well, because the innate talent panel of Levi introduced that only by personally killing an extraordinary professional and absorbing the soul power, could Energy Essence points be generated.

And Levi needed Energy Essence to awaken talents, though it was random—Levi didn't know what innate talent would be awakened next.

At the same time, upgrading innate talents required Energy Essence.

Other people's innate talents improved along with their strength, while Levi needed to use Energy Essence points to upgrade them.

Therefore, Energy Essence was very important to Levi, and right now, among the thieves ahead, there happened to be one Tier 1 Primary Fighter.

Unfortunately, Levi didn't dare to go; Levi was still just a rookie Apprentice Knight and couldn't beat a Tier 1 Primary Fighter at all.

"Better to just lay low until I reach official knight status!"

And at this moment, in the shrubbery ahead, more than 50 thieves were lying in ambush on both sides, waiting for their prey.

But suddenly seeing cavalry charging over in a combat formation, a small leader saw this and immediately said to the boss:

"Boss, they seem to have discovered us. Should we retreat first? These cavalry's equipment seems very high-quality."

"Bullshit! That lord said these are just mercenaries, all for show. I am a Tier 1 professional; how could I be scared away? Damn it, if you say any more discouraging words, I'll chop you up."

The small leader was immediately terrified and hurriedly tried to appease the boss:

"Yes, yes, yes, Boss is a professional fighter; even official knights are no match for Boss."

Of course, this was pure flattery, because in fact, fighter professionals were generally weaker than knight professionals.

And when the thief leader saw the Knight Squad getting closer and closer, and looking murderous, the leader knew they had indeed been discovered, so the leader didn't wait any longer and gave the order directly:

"Kill them for me! Those who perform well will be heavily rewarded."

After the order was given, the leader charged down first.

The rest of the thieves, seeing this, were all as if injected with chicken blood and charged toward the Knight Squad.

Captain Clay saw that the person charging down first was actually a Tier 1 professional fighter, so he immediately ordered his subordinates to maintain formation, and then led the ten Apprentice Knights to charge.

"Crack!"

Eleven heads flew up.

Just in one exchange, the thieves charging at the very front fell down in their excitement.

"Official Knight!"

The thief leader was shocked, knowing the leader might have been deceived.

But running away at this time would only lead to a faster death, so the thief leader turned vicious, immediately calling the subordinates to keep charging, attempting to break up the opponent's knight formation and then find an opportunity to ambush the leading Official Knight.

And Clay had been staring at the thief leader from start to finish, also waiting for an opportunity.

Soon, the thief leader found an opportunity and prepared to ambush Clay, but was met with a backhand sword swing from Clay.

"Crack!"

The thief leader's head flew high into the air.

The Tier 1 fighter thief leader was instantly beheaded.

This terrified the other thieves, who immediately began to flee in all directions.

But how could two legs outrun four?

In the end, only five or six clever thieves managed to run into the shrubbery; the rest of the thieves were either killed or captured.
