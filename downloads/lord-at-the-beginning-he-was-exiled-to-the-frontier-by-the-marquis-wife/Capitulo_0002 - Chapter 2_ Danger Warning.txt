Chapter 2: Danger Warning

Once Levi accepted the reality of his transmigration, stabilized his mindset, and determined his goals, he asked his personal old servant:

"Old Charlie, where are we now? How much longer until we reach the territory?"

"Huh?"

"Oh, Young Master, we have already arrived in Frostmoon Province. We will reach the Lyon Viscount Territory in two more days. After passing the Lyon Viscount Territory, we will reach Gray Fortress, where a battalion of the Northeast Legion is stationed. After passing through Gray Fortress and traveling for another two or three days, we will reach your territory—the Stormwind Territory." Upon hearing Levi's question, the servant Charlie replied immediately.

"???"

Levi was stunned by Old Charlie's series of twists and turns, and he complained frantically in his heart:

"...Isn't this basically leaving the country? No wonder the original owner was scared to death. This isn't a territory at all; it's clearly a fortress outpost."

Old Charlie was a loyal servant left to the original Levi by his mother back in the Baron Louis Territory. Because the original owner's mother passed away early and his father, Baron Louis, was extremely busy, the original Levi was practically raised by Old Charlie. Therefore, even after Levi was driven out of the Baron Louis Territory and exiled to the dangerous borderlands after offending the Tulip Duchess, Old Charlie remained by his side, never leaving him. He was the only person Levi could fully trust at the moment.

After answering Levi's question, Old Charlie suddenly realized something and said excitedly:

"Young Master, are you okay now?"

"I'm fine. You've had a hard time lately!" Levi replied, taking the opportunity to comfort Old Charlie.

Levi knew that after the original owner learned the location of the territory, he had remained in a semi-comatose, dazed state. Even when he woke up a few times, he never spoke. Now that he was suddenly speaking, it was no wonder Old Charlie was so excited.

Seeing that Levi was truly alright, Old Charlie said with tears in his eyes:

"It's not hard at all, as long as you are okay, Young Master."

"However, now that you have woken up, Young Master, let's not go to that damned Stormwind Territory. That place is far too dangerous; I heard several barons have already died there. The people in the royal capital must have made a mistake with the territory. Lady Tulip Duchess could not have possibly assigned you such a dangerous territory. Let's return to the royal capital now and clear things up."

Levi shook his head. Going back was impossible. Through the original owner's memories of rumors regarding his current wife, the Tulip Duchess, he knew that his legal wife, Angelis Tulip, was the only daughter of the late Marquis of Tulip. After the old Marquis passed away, she defeated the other uncles in the family and successfully secured the title of Marquis of Tulip. Such a woman was definitely not a pushover. Moreover, the original Levi was merely a pawn in her hands that could be discarded at any time.

Because Angelis was already 21 years old, family rules dictated that she had to marry. Naturally, her marriage would become a political bargaining chip for her marquis title. Various great nobles and even the royal family—any ambitious person who wanted to interfere with the Tulip Family or swallow up their interests—were all eyeing the position of Angelis's husband. It was the same within the Tulip Family; those family members who wanted to seize power would also try to find a way to start with Angelis's husband. Therefore, the candidate for Angelis's husband became a target for ambitious people to fight over.

Under these circumstances, Angelis suddenly found a second son from a rural baronial family to marry, disrupting the beautiful dreams of all the ambitious people. Consequently, the original Levi became a thorn in the side of all those ambitious people. It was likely that the original owner's inappropriate behavior at the wedding banquet was also someone's doing—and it was very likely Angelis herself. Regardless, the marriage was a done deal. Having Levi around was somewhat of an eyesore, and it saved others from continuing to scheme against him. So, she simply designed his exile. The fact that she paid a high price to get Levi a baronial title was the best proof. One had to know that in the Alpha Kingdom, titles were very difficult to obtain.

Once Levi possessed a baronial title, he could no longer hold any rights within the Tulip Family territory. Because according to kingdom regulations, one person could not exercise rights over two territories; this was a policy specifically set by the kingdom to combat the phenomenon of territory annexation.

However, the location of this territory was likely someone else's doing. Assassinating Levi would give Angelis more excuses to attack or refuse to remarry. But what if Levi brought it upon himself? If he tried to play the hero and ran off to the border territory, wanting to perform meritorious service to please Angelis, and ended up getting himself killed at the border, in that situation, Angelis would have nothing to say and would have to choose another husband.

Therefore, if Levi went back now, it would be equivalent to giving up his title. In that case, even Angelis would likely not let him off. Of course, he didn't want to go to any Stormwind Territory either; that place was equally too dangerous. So, Levi was currently thinking about running away. With the modern knowledge he possessed, if he ran to a relatively stable place, he should be able to develop quite well.

But just as Levi was thinking about running away, a danger warning suddenly appeared in his mind, and at the same time, a simple panel appeared before his eyes:

Name: Levi Louis. Basic Category: Flame Breathing Technique: Beginner Apprentice Knight (79/100). Flame Triple Slash: Beginner (8/10). Horsemanship: Beginner (3/10). Attribute Points: 2. Special Category: Super Precognition: Primary Precognition (0/100): Passively predict danger once daily, range 1 kilometer, duration three days, and can sense others' malice toward you at close range. Energy Essence: 0.

"!!!"

"...Is it finally here? The standard equipment for a transmigrator—a Golden Finger!"
