# CAPÍTULO 1: Chapter 1 Strange Treasure, Residual Root
# Motor: Gemini (gemini-3.5-flash-lite)
############################################################


Yue Kingdom, Lan Province border.

Deep within a remote mountain ravine filled with jagged rocks and tangled vines.

A narrow fissure, perfectly concealed, connected to a dry and refreshing cave.

Inside the cave, a youth in his late twenties withdrew his hands from the midnight-noon hand seal, lifted them from his knees to his chest, and slowly concluded his cultivation.

He let out a long breath of turbid air accumulated in his chest, his breath lingering and deep.

"It is done, Late-Stage Qi Refining Level 7!"

After five years of struggle and hard cultivation, he finally stepped past the threshold of Late-Stage Qi Refining from a mere mortal.

Feeling the Magical Power within his Dantian, which had doubled compared to the sixth level, a trace of joy appeared on Lin Feng's slightly weary face.

Five years. For a rogue cultivator without any Foundation or Inheritance, fending entirely for oneself, the hardships involved could not be finished even if spoken of for days and nights.

The sinister nature of killing people and seizing treasures, the panic of being hunted down, fighting bloody battles over a few low-tier Spirit Stones, and wasting months of time for a superficial fragmented Cultivation Technique... All these were common occurrences.

His face bore traces of weathering unbefitting his age, and deep within his eyes lay the caution and slipperiness unique to rogue cultivators, alongside a trace of helplessness constantly polished away by reality.

"Next year is the Tainan Small Gathering, followed by the Immortal Ascension Assembly... and the Seven Sects accepting disciples..." The youth sighed, carrying a heavy pressure in his heart.

Being a rogue cultivator in the early stages was too difficult; he wondered if he could enter a Sect to muddle along.

His name was Lin Feng. Five years ago, he was still a physics graduate student on Blue Star. While conducting an experiment on quantum entanglement in the laboratory, he encountered an accidental massive explosion, and his true body inexplicably underwent Transmigration into this world, sustaining some injuries.

Fortunately, his luck was good at the time, and he was rescued by a rogue cultivator.

During the process of Transmigration, perhaps due to quantum entanglement or some other reason, his body acquired Spiritual Roots—specifically, Metal, Fire, and Earth tri-Spiritual Roots.

Seeing that he possessed "true" Spiritual Roots, the rogue cultivator who saved him overlooked his slightly advanced age, led him through the door of cultivation, and prepared to take him as a disciple.

Unfortunately, before Lin Feng could truly learn any skills, that rogue cultivator died in a treasure-hunting trip.

However, it was precisely because of that brief contact of a month or two that Lin Feng realized what kind of world he was in.

A Record of a mortal's Journey to Immortality! It was actually the world of A Record of a mortal's Journey to Immortality.

This novel was from his previous life—no, he hadn't died all along, so he couldn't call it a previous life.

In his spare time when he was studying before, he had found this novel utterly unputdownable and had read it several times out of interest.

Naturally, he knew this was a world where people could fly through the skies, escape the earth, move mountains, and overturn seas; it was even more of a world where the weak were prey to the strong, Experts were revered, and jungle Laws were followed.

He also knew that Old Devil Han existed here, and even more... the Palm Heaven Bottle!

Although he had crossed over, he had never discovered that he had a Golden Finger, so during that initial period, he had more than once wanted to plot against this supreme treasure.

Although he knew that Old Devil Han might be the Dao Ancestor reincarnated, as long as the Palm Heaven Bottle hadn't fallen into Old Devil's hands yet, he wasn't without a chance.

Regrettably, when he went through all sorts of hardships to rush to the Seven Mysteries Sect, he discovered that Old Devil Han's Cultivation Base was already higher than his.

At that time, he was at Level 2 Qi Refining, while the other party was already at Qi Refining Level 4. At that time, although Old Devil didn't understand anything, he was absolutely self-taught when it came to fighting and skirmishing, and was fiercely powerful. He simply couldn't beat him, so he had no choice but to retreat in dismay.

Recalling the situation back then, there was still a trace of rejoicing in his heart; fortunately, he hadn't done anything to offend Old Devil, and fortunately, he had been decisive back then, quietly leaving without meeting Old Devil, otherwise a malicious label would definitely have been pinned in Old Devil's heart.

Ever since then, he had lived the bitter life of a rogue cultivator.

Speaking of which, in five years, cultivating from Entry-Level to Late-Stage Qi Refining Level 7 was considered extremely fast among rogue cultivators.

This wasn't entirely due to hard work; it also benefited from that unique trait deep within his soul.

He seemed to have a superhuman comprehension of complex execution trajectories and structures innately. Whether cultivating Cultivation Techniques or practicing spells, he could always find the key points faster and operate and cast them in a more efficient and Magical Power-saving manner.

By this time, he realized that this advantage might be his Golden Finger, or rather, his Talent.

Relying on this Talent and utilizing limited resources, he had finally reached this step today.

Only by entering Late-Stage Qi Refining, the accumulation of Magical Power required for the improvement of each subsequent level would far exceed that of the early stages.

No matter how hard he tried, he still couldn't catch this wave of the Immortal Ascension Assembly. Next time... next time the Demonic Path would almost be attacking. If he joined the Seven Sects at that time, wouldn't that be equivalent to joining that party in 1949.

It seemed that after seizing the first pot of gold from Upper Immortal Jinguang, the treasure-sending boy, he should make other plans. A trace of bitterness welled up at the corners of Lin Feng's mouth.

Right at this moment, perhaps because he had just broken through his Cultivation Realm, his Cultivation Base had just stabilized, and at the instant when his Divine Soul tended to become active after growing.

"Buzz!" A familiar yet incomparably bizarre palpitation suddenly burst forth from the deepest part of his soul.

"It's here, I've finally waited for it!" He had waited three years for this feeling.

Because this kind of situation had also appeared once when he broke through to Qi Refining Level 4.

That time, his consciousness was pulled by a mysterious force, and in a trance, he touched a distant and shattered world, grabbing a small metal fragment containing special energy from within.

The fragment was later integrated by him into a Magical Artifact, causing this Magical Artifact's grade not to improve—it was still an intermediate-grade Magical Artifact—but its might reached the level of a top-grade Magical Artifact. Moreover, using it consumed Divine Sense and Magical Power basically no different from an intermediate-grade Magical Artifact.

The Magical Artifact was named "White Rainbow" by him. In his cultivation career over the past few years, it had saved him from danger several times and helped him seize resources.

At this moment, the same feeling descended once more, and it was faintly clearer than last time. His consciousness was forcibly pulled away and thrown into a bizarre and mottled passage.

Surrounding him were rapidly flowing shattered lights and shadows, colorful turbulent currents, and bubbles. Here, he had a feeling of blurred time and space, always feeling mysterious and chaotic.

The huge tearing sensation seemed to act directly upon his Divine Soul, bringing waves of dizziness and stinging pain.

His "vision" was limited and extremely unstable, and he could only vaguely "see" a boundary rapidly approaching and quickly moving away.

That was a scene of desolate and dilapidated ruins.

Scorched earth, fractured mountain ranges, overturned giant palace wreckage, dried and cracked riverbeds... There were no signs of life, only endless desolation.

The entire process was too fast for thought, and the connection between his consciousness and the ruins was rapidly weakening, with the possibility of being bounced back at any moment.

Lin Feng had no time to carefully explore. Relying entirely on his previous experience and an instinctive intuition, he concentrated all his mind and fiercely grabbed towards the only place in the ruins where he sensed a slightly "abnormal" fluctuation!

The next moment, the world spun.

"Ugh!" Inside the Cave Dwelling, Lin Feng let out a muffled groan, his body swaying side to side. His complexion instantly turned as pale as paper, and fine cold sweat oozed from his forehead.

A strong sense of Divine Sense overdraft struck, making his vision darken and almost causing him to faint.

Just like last time, performing such a "grabbing" action placed an extremely heavy burden on the Divine Soul.

Suppressing his discomfort, he laboriously raised his right hand and slowly opened his palm. Inside his palm, a "thing" quietly lay.

Staring intently at it, Lin Feng's heart couldn't help but sink slightly.

That was a withered wood root about three inches long and thinner than a little finger. Entirely scorched black, as if it had been scorched by a blazing fire, its surface was covered with twisted textures, withered and fragile, seeming as if it would completely turn into flying ash with a gentle pinch.

One end was even stained with a little similarly scorched soil. Overall, it looked like something left behind after a certain shrub had been struck by lightning and scorched by fire.

"Just this?"

Compared to the metal fragment from last time, which was of unknown Origin but at least flashed with a strange luster, the appearance of the scorched withered wood root before him was far too poor, practically resembling charcoal raked out from a stove.

A wave of disappointment welled up in his heart. Could this rare Divine Soul contact be exchanged for just such a piece of junk?

Lin Feng sighed, but still prepared to circulate his Magical Power and Divine Sense to investigate.

However, just at the moment when his Magical Power was about to touch that withered wood root, a sudden change occurred!

The scorched textures on the surface of the withered wood root lit up slightly. Immediately following, an indescribable aura, seemingly carrying vitality and the passage of years, permeated from inside the withered wood root.

Although this aura was weak, Lin Feng could genuinely feel it.

His body involuntarily stiffened, the surging Magical Power abruptly halting, and then he cautiously covered it with his Divine Sense.

The instant his Divine Sense made contact with that wisp of aura, an unspeakable and indescribable feeling turned into a piece of information that emerged in his mind:

[Unknown Divine Wood Residual Root]: Internally contains a trace of Xiantian Origin Qi of Wood so weak it is nearly dissipated. Its breath can nourish all things, greatly accelerating the growth of Spirit Plants, and can also slightly nourish the Divine Soul and purify Magical Power.

The information was brief, but it exploded in Lin Feng's mind like a thunderclap.

Xiantian Origin Qi of Wood? Accelerating the growth of Spirit Plants! Nourishing the Divine Soul, purifying Magical Power!

Lin Feng's eyes suddenly widened, his pupils contracted, his breathing instantly became extremely rapid, and the hand holding the withered wood root even trembled slightly due to extreme excitement.

He abruptly clenched his palm, as if afraid this treasure would grow wings and fly away, but then realized its fragility, quickly relaxing his strength and carefully holding it with both hands.

His heart pounded wildly, and the sound of surging blood seemed to echo in his ears.

A treasure, it was definitely a heaven-sent treasure! Doesn't this thing have the same utility as the Palm Heaven Bottle! It might even be stronger than that, since this item also had the effect of nourishing the Divine Soul and purifying Magical Power.

"No, there is a description of 'fast dissipating' inside. Fuck, won't it be gone before I even use it?"

Lin Feng's ecstatic heart quickly calmed down. Such a good treasure had to be stored properly, and then used in a hurry; perhaps it would be gone soon after.

He swiftly rummaged through his Storage Bag to find a blank jade box used for storing precious items, and carefully placed the withered wood root—which looked scorched and unremarkable, but was actually of immeasurable value—into the jade box.

The moment he closed the box lid, a sudden feeling arose in his heart, and he looked toward the corner of the Cave Dwelling.

A few ordinary hemostatic herbs grew casually there, which he had casually transplanted back when he was injured outside, and they were usually half-dead.

Shortly after the jade box was closed, the originally withered and drooping leaves of those few hemostatic herbs actually stretched out slightly at a speed visible to the naked eye, and their color also became a fraction more verdant.

Effective, it was truly effective! This Xiantian Origin Qi of Wood could indeed promote the growth of vegetation.

Lin Feng tightly grasped the jade box, his knuckles turning slightly white from the force.

A plan quickly formed in his mind: purchase Spirit Herb seeds or seedlings, use the withered wood root to ripen them rapidly, exchange them for Spirit Stones, and then purchase Medicinal Pills, cooperating with the withered wood root's Magical Power-purifying effect, to push for a higher Cultivation Realm with all his might.

He took a deep breath, suppressing his surging emotions. The more it was at a time like this, the more caution was needed; he must not reveal any flaws.

He solemnly placed the jade box into the Storage Bag, put it away close to his body, pushed away the concealing stone at the cave entrance, and the external daylight came into view.

Lin Feng took a step forward, his figure rapidly disappearing into the verdant mountain forests.

Over a hundred miles away from the mountain stream where Lin Feng was in seclusion, there was a small marketplace.

This marketplace was not directly controlled by any major sect or faction; instead, it had spontaneously formed over many years through the operations of small shops belonging to several cultivation Clans.

Over time, it attracted some rogue cultivators to come and trade. Although the scale was not large, its advantage lay in its low tier, meaning the profits naturally weren't too high, and formidable figures generally did not frequent it. For a rogue cultivator like Lin Feng, it was actually much more convenient and safer.

Stepping once again into this remote and sparsely populated marketplace street, Lin Feng's state of mind was completely different from before.

In the past, he mostly came here to sell low-grade Spirit Herbs or Demonic Beast materials that he had gathered at great risk, exchanging them for just a few Spirit Stones to buy cultivation supplies.

Every transaction was meticulously haggled over, and his heart was weighed down with confusion about the future.

But today, harboring hope for the future, his steady pace revealed an irrepressible eagerness.

However, when he stepped onto the street, his mind suddenly gave a start. With a shrug of his shoulders, his entire demeanor reverted to its usual appearance, ensuring that no one could detect anything unusual.

He was in no rush to make purchases. Instead, he leisurely strolled around the marketplace twice, his gaze seemingly sweeping casually across various stalls and shops, while actually closely observing the prices of various Spirit Herb seeds or seedlings, as well as the market conditions of finished Spirit Herbs.

Especially common Spirit Herbs like Huangjingzhi and Ziyehua, which were needed for refining Medicinal Pills to increase the Cultivation Base of the Qi Refining Stage.

The demand for such Spirit Herbs was stable, and the age requirements were relatively lenient. Generally, they could be used as medicine after only a few decades, making them a popular choice for planting and trading among rogue cultivators and Clans.

Their price fluctuations were small, but for Lin Feng, who possessed the Origin of Wood Qi, the profit margin could well be described as exorbitant.

Most importantly, during the next step of selling them, it would not arouse unwarranted speculation from others.

"Fellow Daoist, would you like to take a look at these newly arrived talisman? They are extremely powerful, and are essential for staying at home, traveling, killing—ah no, for self-defense! Would you like to buy one?" a middle-aged male stall owner greeted enthusiastically.

Lin Feng merely shook his head faintly and did not stop his footsteps.

The Spirit Stones he had earned over the years were mainly used for cultivation to enhance his Cultivation Base, and he only had about thirty-odd left in his pocket.

This sum of Spirit Stones was not a small amount for a rogue cultivator, but for his plan, it appeared stretched to the limit.

Not long after, he walked into an old shop named "Hundred Herbs Hall".

The storefront of this shop was not large, but its goods were relatively comprehensive. The boss was an old man at the twelfth layer of the Qi Refining Stage, whose character was considered fair, and at least there were rare rumors of the shop bullying customers with its size.

"Shopkeeper, how are the seeds of Huangjingzhi and Ziyehua sold?" Lin Feng asked in a low voice.

The old shopkeeper raised his eyelids and looked Lin Feng up and down: "The seeds are cheap, one Spirit Stone per pack, and one pack is enough to plant ten plants; the seedlings are a bit more expensive, two plants for one Spirit Stone. Is Fellow Daoist planning to cultivate them yourself?"

Lin Feng lowered his eyelids. The price difference between the two was fivefold. The seeds were cheap, but there was also a probability of germination, and growing into seedlings took time. Even with the accelerated withered wood root, it would not skip this stage.

Seedlings could bypass the germination stage and yield results faster, allowing him to test the effect of the withered wood root.

However, he could take some of both.

"Give me two packs of each seed, and six seedlings of each as well." Lin Feng made a prompt decision.

"Alrighty, that will be ten Spirit Stones."

The old shopkeeper skillfully took out two small wooden basins from the wooden rack behind him, each containing six drooping seedlings whose leaves were somewhat withered and yellow.

From two drawers, he then took out two small cloth bags each.

Lin Feng paid the Spirit Stones and asked casually, as if on a whim: "Shopkeeper, do you have any extra Spirit Plant pots here? The cheaper kind will do."

The old shopkeeper glanced at him and smiled: "Is Fellow Daoist planning to open up a medicine garden? We do have them. The lowest-grade Spirit Gathering Basin comes with a miniature Spirit Gathering effect, which can save you a lot of tending effort. It's ten Spirit Stones each."

The corners of Lin Feng's mouth twitched slightly. How expensive!

But the Origin Qi inside that withered wood root was on the verge of collapsing; adding a bit of Spiritual Qi might have a delaying effect.

Gritting his teeth, he paid another ten Spirit Stones and took a grayish pottery basin about the size of a washbasin, with a simple Formation engraved at the bottom.

At this point, he only had three Spirit Stones left on him.

After thinking for a moment, he used this last bit of Spirit Stones to purchase some common nutrient solution needed for cultivating Spirit Herbs.

Walking out of Hundred Herbs Hall, the Spirit Stones in Lin Feng's Storage Bag were completely empty.

Although it felt like he had been instantly set back to before liberation, it held his hopes for the future.

Returning to the Cave Dwelling in the desolate mountain stream, Lin Feng immediately got busy.

First, he carefully placed the Spirit Plant basin in a relatively Spiritual Qi-dense corner inside the Cave Dwelling, then diluted the purchased nutrient solution and poured a little into the basin.

Using a jade knife, he dug a small pit in the soft soil at the center of the Spirit Plant basin.

Then, he solemnly took out the snow-white jade box and opened the lid. That charred withered wood root lay quietly inside.

He took a deep breath, gently pinched that section of withered wood root with two fingers, placed it vertically into the pit, and slowly covered it with soil, leaving only a small section of about half an inch exposed at the top.

After finishing all this, he stepped back two paces and nervously watched the Spirit Plant basin.

At first, there was no abnormality inside.

Just as Lin Feng was feeling somewhat apprehensive, the mysterious patterns on the surface of the charred withered wood root imperceptibly lit up for a split second once again.

Immediately afterward, an illusory and strange aura began to slowly permeate outward with the withered wood root as the center.

An unspeakable sense of vitality quietly grew.

Lin Feng's eyes shone, and he immediately carefully transplanted the twelve Spirit Herb seedlings into the soil around the withered wood root.

After thinking about it, he sprinkled the four packs of Huangjingzhi and Ziyehua seeds onto the soil and poured all the remaining nutrient solution over them.

Soon, a miracle happened!

Those seedlings, which had originally become somewhat limp and soft after leaving the soil for a period of time, unexpectedly straightened up slowly under the nourishment of the Origin of Xiantian Wood Qi.

Their leaves unfolded, and the yellowish part that was originally mixed with green slowly turned light green. Then, the entire leaf became emerald green, faintly revealing a trace of oily luster.

The visibly growing momentum made Lin Feng's heart wild with joy.

However, he knew this was only the beginning, and what came next was the main point.

Thus, he sat cross-legged beside the Spirit Plant basin, practicing his Cultivation Technique to recover the Magical Power consumed by today's running around, while splitting a portion of his mind to closely monitor the changes inside the Spirit Plant basin.

Under the shroud of the Origin of Wood Qi, his efficiency in absorbing Spiritual Qi seemed to have improved just a tiny bit compared to before.

More importantly, when the Spiritual Qi inhaled into his body circulated through his meridians, it seemed to have been filtered by this aura, becoming gentler and purer, and blending much more smoothly into the Magical Power vortex of his Dantian.

"This is the effect of purifying Magical Power?" Recalling the introduction in that information, Lin Feng operated his Cultivation Technique even more earnestly.

Time passed day by day.

Lin Feng almost never left the Cave Dwelling, with most of his mind tethered to those twelve Spirit Herbs and the withered wood root in the center.

Every day, those Spirit Herbs underwent significant changes.

Their leaves grew thicker and fatter, their stalks grew sturdier, and their height increased slowly but steadily.

In a mere seven or eight days, the Spirit Herbs had grown to the extent of ten to twenty years.

As for the section of withered wood root in the center, its appearance remained the same as before, but the aura it emitted imperceptibly weakened by a tiny fraction.

Lin Feng was both gratified and worried; gratified that it could continue to work, and worried about how long its "near-death" state could be maintained.

One month later.

The scenery inside the Spirit Plant basin had completely changed.

The twelve Spirit Herbs were no longer seedlings, but had become true Spirit Plants in the real sense.

Huangjingzhi unfolded its signature heavy leaves, and Ziyehua also sprouted winding short branches with dark red leaves, emitting a faint medicinal fragrance.

Judging by his experience, this medicinal age... probably already exceeded ninety years, or even approached a hundred years, completely reaching the stage of maturity for medicinal use.

As for those seeds, seventy to eighty percent of them had taken root and germinated, and he transplanted them into several other ordinary pottery basins.

Moreover, their medicinal age basically reached the standard of eighty to ninety years. Although slightly inferior to those over a hundred years old when used as medicine, it was sufficient.

Consequently, he instantly possessed forty mature Spirit Herbs that could be used to refine Medicinal Pills to increase the Cultivation Base of the Qi Refining Stage.

However, after a month's time, the Origin of Wood Qi emitted by that section of withered wood root could already be distinctly felt to have weakened somewhat.

Lin Feng dared not wait any longer. Firstly, he was afraid that prolonged nights would lead to many dreams; secondly, he worried that the withered wood root could not hold out much longer.

He carefully dug out all the Spirit Herbs and kept them properly stored.

Arriving at the marketplace once again, Lin Feng did not go to "Hundred Herbs Hall" this time. Instead, he chose a day when the crowd was slightly larger and sold these Spirit Herbs to three different shops separately.

The process was even smoother than he had imagined. Although the shopkeepers of these shops were somewhat surprised that he could suddenly produce a dozen Spirit Herbs of good appearance and sufficient medicinal age, they did not delve too deeply into it.

rogue cultivators always had their own fated opportunities—either discovering some predecessor's medicine garden or having dog-shit luck to harvest wild Spirit Herbs. As long as the goods were fine, no one would dig to the bottom of it.

Forty Spirit Herbs in total traded him a full 570 low-grade Spirit Stones.

However, he spent another hundred-odd Spirit Stones purchasing a large number of Spirit Herb seeds across the three shops, many of which were Spirit Herb seeds that could be used to refine Medicinal Pills to enhance the Cultivation Base of Foundation Establishment cultivators.

For example, Baihezhi, moon-gazing grass, and so on.

These seeds were much more expensive than the previous Huangjingzhi; a single pack of seeds actually sold for eight Spirit Stones, and they were reduced to a pack of five grains.

Truly fucking treacherous merchants and dark merchants!

Of course, he also purchased a small number of seedlings of the previous two Spirit Herbs to serve as a smoke bomb to conceal his true purpose.

As for the Spirit Plant basin used to cultivate Spirit Herbs, he also made a heavy investment to purchase one with a larger capacity and a better Spirit Gathering effect, which cost him a full fifty-odd Spirit Stones.

Afterwards, harboring the remaining three hundred-odd Spirit Stones, Lin Feng came to "Danxiang Pavilion", the only shop in the marketplace that sold Medicinal Pills.

"Shopkeeper, how are Qi Gathering Pills sold?" Lin Feng asked directly.

Qi Gathering Pills were the Medicinal Pills most commonly used by Late-Stage Qi Refining Cultivators to refine and advance their Cultivation Base.

"Five pills per bottle, thirty Spirit Stones," the shopkeeper replied.

"I... I'll take ten bottles." Lin Feng was hesitant, his face aching with extreme pain.

In reality, being this extravagant for the first time, his heart was also bleeding; the five hundred-odd Spirit Stones he had just harvested instantly dwindled down to only twenty or thirty blocks left.

But there was no help for it. If he wanted to rapidly enhance his Cultivation Base, he had to purchase Medicinal Pills. That today's shop could provide so many Medicinal Pills could also be considered his good luck.

Under the circumstances where Spirit Herb resources were scarce in the macro environment, Qi Refining Stage Spirit Herbs were easier to talk about; they could be slowly cultivated over decades.

When it came to the Foundation Establishment Stage, there was basically no such good thing anymore. There were almost no Medicinal Pills for sale on the market that enhanced Foundation Establishment cultivation, relying entirely on personal transactions. Many Foundation Establishment cultivators traveled and searched for several years without necessarily being able to buy a single bottle of Medicinal Pills.

After settling the Spirit Stones and Medicinal Pills, Lin Feng strolled around other shops in the marketplace for a bit.

He sold all the items on his body that he wouldn't need in the short term, and combined with the remaining twenty or thirty Spirit Stones, he purchased a set of Formation with early warning, shielding, and simple defensive functions.

Only then did he cautiously and vigilantly leave the marketplace.
